Overview

Why Modularity and Interoperability?

Scaling in-space missions into commercial services will require a new generation of autonomous robots capable of operating across diverse platforms, missions, and service providers. Achieving this vision depends on embedding modularity and interoperability into the design, development, and operation of space robots and supporting infrastructure.

While significant advances have been made in robotic manipulation, autonomous operations, and servicing technologies, the absence of common interfaces, standards, and interoperable architectures remains a major barrier to scalability and commercial adoption.

This workshop will identify key technical, operational, and regulatory challenges and explore opportunities for standardization and cross-sector collaboration — with a focus on how modular and interoperable robotic ecosystems can reduce costs, increase flexibility, enable sustainable space operations, and unlock a thriving in-space economy.

Index Terms — Autonomous systems, interoperability, in-orbit services, modularity, space robotics

Panel Discussion

The town-hall panel will bring together expert panelists and the audience to explore technical, operational, commercial, and policy challenges. Particular attention will be given to common interfaces, standards, certification approaches, business incentives, and the collaborative frameworks required to transition from proprietary solutions towards open, scalable ecosystems.

Early Career Researchers

Call for Poster Presentations

Early-career researchers are invited to present their latest research through 3-minute poster pitches at the workshop. Selected participants will deliver a concise pitch highlighting the novelty, relevance, and potential impact of their work. Optional "fast-break" video presentations are also welcome.

Posters will be evaluated by a multidisciplinary panel from industry, academia, and space agencies. Key selection criteria are direct relevance to the workshop topic, with equity, diversity, and inclusion actively considered.

The best poster will be awarded the Best Poster Award at the workshop close.
