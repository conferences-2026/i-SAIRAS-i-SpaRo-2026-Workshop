Workshop Overview

About the Workshop

As space exploration moves towards sustained orbital servicing, manufacturing, assembly (ISAM), and long-term planetary exploration, modularity and interoperability have emerged as critical enablers. Current space robotic systems remain largely custom-built, proprietary, and mission-specific — limiting reusability, increasing costs, and hindering multi-agent collaboration.

This workshop aims to address these challenges by creating a shared platform for space agencies, research institutions, and commercial space actors. Building upon international initiatives such as i-SpaRo, CONFERS, and European/Asian modularity frameworks, we will discuss standardization of mechanical, electrical, data, and software interfaces.

"Connecting international efforts to transition space robotics from isolated, single-use systems to sustainable, plug-and-play ecosystems."

Key Themes & Focus Areas

  • Standardized mechanical, electrical, & data interfaces (HOTDOCK, SIOM, SIAD, etc.)
  • Modular robotic arms, end-effectors, and tool-exchangers
  • Software architectures & middleware interoperability (ROS2/Space, cFS, TASTE)
  • In-Space Servicing, Assembly, and Manufacturing (ISAM) workflows
  • Regulatory, commercial, and agency-level harmonization
  • Verification, validation, and ground testing for modular systems
